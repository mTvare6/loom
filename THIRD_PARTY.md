# Third-party data

## MIT KEMAR HRTF measurements

The four impulse responses in `full/elev0` are taken from the full MIT KEMAR
HRTF measurement set created by Bill Gardner and Keith Martin at the MIT Media
Lab in 1994. They represent both ears at 0 degrees elevation and azimuths 30
and 330 degrees.

Copyright 1994 MIT Media Laboratory. The dataset is provided free with no
restrictions on use, provided its authors are cited in research or commercial
applications.

Source and documentation:
<https://sound.media.mit.edu/resources/KEMAR.html>

Suggested citation: Bill Gardner and Keith Martin, "HRTF Measurements of a
KEMAR Dummy-Head Microphone," MIT Media Lab, 1994.
